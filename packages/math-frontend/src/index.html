Welcome to XivGear!

xivgear.app is a brand-new FFXIV gear calculator and planner. Build, compare, calculate, and simulate your sets without complicated spreadsheets.

This section of XivGear contains mathematical tables and formulae. You can click a section at the top to see the calculator and formulae for that section.
